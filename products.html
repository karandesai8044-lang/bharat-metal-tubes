Complete Product Range

We take this opportunity to introduce ourselves as one of the leading importers, stockist & suppliers of the following materials.

1. Stainless Steel

SS 202, 304, 304L, 310, 316, 316L, 317L, 321, 410, 904L — welded & seamless pipe and tubes confirming to A 312 / A 213, plus pipe, sheet, plate, round, square, hex, wire bar, angle, channel and patta-patti.

2. SS Fittings & Flanges

SS 304 / 316 welded & seamless pipe-fittings (B.W. / S.W. / screwed) confirming to A-403 / A 182F, and flanges confirming to A240 / A 182F.

3. C.S. / F.C.S. Fittings & Flanges

A234 / A105 / G.I. (ISI) pipe fittings (B.W. / S.W. / screwed) and C.S. (IS 2062) / F.C.S. (A105) flanges — slip-on, blind, socket weld and weld-neck, in IBR & non-IBR.

4. M.S. & G.I. ERW Pipes

TATA / ZENITH / JINDAL make M.S. and G.I. ERW pipes as per IS 1239 and IS 3589 Fe 330 / 410.

5. C.S. Seamless Pipes & Boiler Tubes

TATA / MSL / ISMT / imported origin C.S. seamless pipes confirming to A 106 Gr. B / SA 179 / DIN 17175 / A-333 Gr. 6 / API-5L, and ERW / seamless boiler tubes as per BS 3059.

6. Polypropylene & H.D.P.E.

PP & HDPE pipe-fittings and flanges, PP moulded ball valves, non-return valves, foot valves, sight glass and diaphragm valves.

7. Industrial Valves

All types of ball valves in C.I. / C.C.S. / C.S.S. construction & S.S. bar stock; gate, globe, check, needle, safety relief, foot, flush bottom, butterfly, pulp, pinch, diaphragm, plug and strainer valves.

8. Steam & Flow Accessories

C.C.S / C.S.S. / F.C.S / F.S.S. gate, globe & non-return valves, M.S. & S.S. sight flow indicators, C.I. float / bucket type steam traps and thermic fluid valves.

9. Plates & Conveyor Items

SS sheet, plate & coil, Hardox plate 400 / 500 / 600 grade, BQ plate, Sail Hard plate, Jindal Hard plate, all types of conveyor items and roller idler pulley (idler frame).

All the above items can be supplied with IBR & Govt. approved lab test certificates, under approval by any third-party inspection agency.
