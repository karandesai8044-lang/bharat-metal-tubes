Who We Are

One of the Leading Importers, Stockist & Suppliers of Ferrous & Non-Ferrous Metals

Bharat Metal & Tubes is a Raipur (Chhattisgarh) based importer, stockist and supplier of all types of industrial raw materials in ferrous and non-ferrous metal. We are also manufacturers of stainless steel pipes in all grades, shapes and sizes.

We have technical know-how in this trade and are well versed in project and maintenance supply to various organisations in chemicals, fertilisers, petrochemicals, nuclear power, thermal power, cement, paper and refinery sectors — catering to their requirements on a repeat-order basis with quality products on convenient terms.
