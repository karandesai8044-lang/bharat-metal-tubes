Who We Are

Leading Importers, Stockist & Suppliers of Ferrous & Non-Ferrous Metals

Based in Bhanpuri, Raipur (Chhattisgarh), Bharat Metal & Tubes is one of the leading importers, stockist and suppliers of all types of Ferrous and Non-Ferrous metals. We are also manufacturers of stainless steel pipes in all grades, shapes and sizes. With technical know-how in this trade, we are well versed in project and maintenance supply to chemicals, fertilisers, petrochemicals, nuclear power, thermal power, cement, paper and refinery industries.
