Weight Formulas

Formula of Weight

Use these standard formulas to calculate material weight — or try our automatic calculator.

Stainless Steel

Pipes & Tubes(O.D. − W.T.) × W.T. × 0.02466 = kg/metre
Round BarDia × Dia × 0.00623 = kg/metre
Square BarDia × Dia × 0.00787 = kg/metre
Hexagonal RodDia × Dia × 0.00680 = kg/metre
Flat BarWidth × Thk × 0.00798 = kg/metre
SheetLength × Width × Thk × 8 = kg/sheet
Circle & BlankDia × Dia × Thk ÷ 160 = grams/pc

Other Metals

Brass / Copper Pipe(Length − W.T.) × W.T. × 0.0260 = kg/metre
Carbon Steel SheetLength × Width × Thk × 7.85 = kg/sheet
Lead Pipe(O.D. − W.T.) × W.T. × 0.0345 = kg/metre
Aluminium Pipe(O.D. − W.T.) × W.T. × 0.0082 = kg/metre
Lead SheetLength × Width × Thk × 11.2 = kg/sheet
Aluminium SheetLength × Width × Thk × 2.69 = kg/sheet

Tensile Strength Conversion

Kg/mm² × 9.81= N/mm² = MPa
Psi × 0.0007= Kg/mm²
KSi × 1000= PSi
Kg/mm² × 1.422= KSi
